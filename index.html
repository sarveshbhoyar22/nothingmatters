FIELD PROTOCOL

NOTHINGMATTER RULES.

Simple agreements that make every weekend magical, safe and completely unpretentious.

01 NON-NEGOTIABLE

DON'T OVERTHINK IT.

The snooze button is a trap. Your mind will try to convince you to stay in bed. Just put your feet on the floor and lace up your sneakers.

"Future you will thank you at 7:00 AM."

02 MINDSET

COME CURIOUS.

Ask questions. Notice the yellow amaltas flowers, the thorny acacia bark, the babbler birds. Look at things you normally rush past in traffic.

"Leave your expert badge at home."

03 SOCIAL

TALK TO PEOPLE.

Introverts are deeply welcomed here. Nobody is here to pitch you a startup or judge your outfit. Say hello to whoever is walking next to you.

"Strangers are just friends you haven't hiked with."

04 DIGITAL DETOX

PHONES DOWN SOMETIMES.

Take one photo if you must, then pocket it. If you spend the sunrise staging reels, you missed the entire point of waking up at dawn.

"Eyes have better resolution than OLED screens."

05 ETHICS

LEAVE THE PLACE BETTER THAN YOU FOUND IT.

Pack out your trash. Pick up a plastic bottle left behind by someone else. Treat the ancient Aravalli ridges like sacred living soil.

"Zero trace. Maximum respect."

06 CORE VIBE

HAVE FUN.

This isn't an Olympic trial or an endurance test. If you want to sit down on a rock and eat a banana while others run, sit down on the rock.

"Nothing matters. Seriously."
